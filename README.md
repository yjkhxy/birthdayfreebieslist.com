## How to plan your birthday rewards

1. **Start before your birthday month.** Each program sets its own enrollment cutoff. Joining on your birthday may be too late; some programs also require prior qualifying activity.
2. **Check the brand guide and its official source.** Look for membership requirements, birthday registration, prior spending, eligible locations and redemption channels. Where shown, a source-check date records a review, not a guarantee.
3. **Compare the full cost.** A free account does not mean a free reward. An online order, delivery charge, minimum spend or paid membership can change the deal.
4. **Confirm the actual account offer.** Check your app, email or member account for the item, activation steps and expiry. Bring the required code or identification.
5. **Plan only confirmed stops.** Check local participation and stock before travelling. Save each offer's deadline rather than assuming every reward lasts all month.

### Birthday reward checklist

Copy this into your notes for each program:

## Frequently asked questions

### Are all birthday rewards really free?

No. Some programs offer a qualifying redemption without an additional purchase; others require prior spending, a purchase with redemption or a paid membership. Some claims remain unconfirmed. Read the conditions for the specific offer and channel.

### Can I sign up on my birthday?

Sometimes, but do not assume so. Programs can require advance enrollment, a birthday saved before a cutoff, or earlier qualifying activity. Use the brand's current rules when planning.

### Can I get birthday freebies by mail?

An emailed coupon or app reward is different from a physical gift shipped to your home. The directory currently does not establish a source-checked physical-mail birthday gift. Its delivery guide explains the evidence and the distinction; an email offer can still require a store visit or purchase.

### Will a listed brand participate near me?

Not necessarily. National program information does not establish local store participation. Confirm with the official program and your intended location before making a trip.

### Does this repository issue rewards?

No. Rewards are issued and redeemed by the relevant brands. This guide helps you compare conditions and find official sources. Do not post account credentials, birthday details, reward codes or receipts containing personal information in public issues.
